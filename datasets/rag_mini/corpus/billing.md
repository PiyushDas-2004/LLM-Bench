# Billing and Fees

This document describes how Lumen Valley Water Cooperative bills members for water service, the current rate structure, and the fees that may apply to an account. The rates below took effect on 1 July 2024 and were approved by the board in March 2024.

## Billing cycle

Meters are read automatically by radio every month, and bills are issued monthly. Each bill is due 21 days after the issue date shown on the bill.

## Rate structure

Every residential bill has two parts: a fixed base charge and a usage charge.

The fixed base charge for a standard residential connection is 18.50 dollars per month. Larger connections used by farms and businesses pay a higher base charge that depends on meter size.

The usage charge is tiered to encourage conservation:

- Tier 1: the first 10 cubic metres each month are charged at 1.20 dollars per cubic metre.
- Tier 2: usage from 10 to 25 cubic metres is charged at 1.85 dollars per cubic metre.
- Tier 3: usage above 25 cubic metres is charged at 2.60 dollars per cubic metre.

## Fees

The following fees may be added to an account:

- Late payment fee: a flat 12 dollars is charged if payment is not received by the due date.
- Returned payment fee: 30 dollars for any cheque or bank debit that is returned unpaid.
- Reconnection fee: 75 dollars during business hours, or 140 dollars for an after-hours reconnection.
- Meter test fee: 45 dollars when a member requests an accuracy test; this fee is refunded if the meter is found to over-register by more than 2 percent.

## Payment assistance

Members experiencing financial hardship may apply for the Helping Hands program, which provides a credit of up to 300 dollars per year toward water bills. Applications are reviewed by the Member Services team within 15 business days. Members may also request a payment plan that spreads an overdue balance over as many as six months without late fees, provided each installment is paid on time.

## Disconnection for non-payment

Service is never disconnected for non-payment on a Friday, on a weekend, or on the day before a public holiday. Before any disconnection, the member receives a written notice at least 14 days in advance and a phone call at least 48 hours in advance.

## Billing questions

If a member believes a bill is wrong, they should contact Member Services within 60 days of the bill date. While a billing dispute is under review, no late fees are applied to the disputed amount.
