# Service Area and New Connections

This document describes the area served by Lumen Valley Water Cooperative and the process for requesting a new water service connection.

## Service area

The cooperative's service area covers about 410 square kilometres of the Lumen Valley. It includes the towns of Brindle and Copper Ford, the village of Orrin Creek, and the surrounding farmland between the Harrow Hills to the east and the Sedge River to the west. Properties outside these boundaries are not eligible for service unless the board approves an extension of the service area.

Within the service area, properties where a water main already runs along the road frontage are in the core zone, and all other properties are in the extension zone.

## Requesting a new connection

To request a new connection, a property owner submits a New Service Application along with a non-refundable application fee of 120 dollars. The engineering department reviews the application to confirm that the system has enough capacity and that the property is inside the service area. The review normally takes 20 business days.

## Connection charges

If the application is approved, the applicant pays a connection charge before work begins. For a standard 20-millimetre residential connection in the core zone, the connection charge is 3,850 dollars, which covers the tap into the main, the service line to the property boundary, and the meter. Larger connections for farms and businesses are priced individually by the engineering department.

The applicant must also purchase a membership share before service is turned on.

## Main extensions

Properties in the extension zone require a main extension. The applicant pays the full cost of designing and building the new main, which must be at least 150 millimetres in diameter. If other properties connect to the extension within 10 years, the original applicant receives a partial refund of the cost from each new connection, known as a latecomer payment.

## Installation timeline

Once the connection charge has been paid, cooperative crews install a standard residential connection within 45 days, weather permitting. Installations are generally not scheduled between 15 December and 15 February because frozen ground makes excavation difficult.

## Property owner responsibilities

The cooperative owns and maintains everything up to and including the meter. The property owner is responsible for the private service line from the meter to the building and for all plumbing inside the building. Every new connection must include a backflow prevention device approved by the cooperative, which the owner must have tested once a year by a certified tester.
