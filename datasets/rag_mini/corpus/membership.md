# Membership Policy

Lumen Valley Water Cooperative is owned by its members. Every customer who holds a water service connection in their own name must also be a member of the cooperative. This policy explains who can join, what membership costs, and what rights and duties come with it.

## Eligibility

Membership is open to any person, household, farm, business or public body that owns or leases property within the cooperative's service area and receives water service from the cooperative. Tenants may become members if their lease is at least 12 months long and the property owner signs a consent form. Each service connection carries exactly one membership; a person who holds three connections holds three memberships but casts only one vote in board elections.

## Membership share

New members must purchase one membership share at a cost of 150 dollars. The share is not a fee: it is held on the member's account and is refunded in full, without interest, when the membership ends and all outstanding bills have been paid. Members who cannot pay the full amount at once may spread the share over three consecutive monthly bills.

## Rights of members

Members have the right to:

- vote in the annual board election and on any proposed change to the bylaws;
- attend the Annual General Meeting, held each year on the second Saturday of May;
- inspect the cooperative's audited financial statements;
- receive patronage credits when the cooperative ends the fiscal year with a surplus.

Patronage credits are allocated in proportion to the amount each member paid for water during the year and are applied as a credit on the member's bill, usually in the September billing cycle.

## Duties of members

Members agree to pay their bills on time, to allow cooperative staff reasonable access to meters and service lines, and to follow any water-use restrictions declared by the board during a drought. A member who repeatedly violates restrictions may be issued a written warning and, after a third warning within one calendar year, a fine of 250 dollars.

## Ending membership

A membership ends when the member sells or vacates the property, or asks for the service connection to be permanently disconnected. To end membership, the member must submit a Membership Termination Form at least 10 business days before the move-out date. The share refund is issued by cheque or bank transfer within 30 days after the final bill is settled.

## Contact

Questions about membership can be directed to the Member Services desk, which is open Monday to Friday from 8:00 to 17:00.
