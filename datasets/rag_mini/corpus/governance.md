# Governance and Board Elections

Lumen Valley Water Cooperative is governed by a Board of Directors elected by and from its members. This document describes the composition of the board, how directors are elected, and how the board conducts its business.

## Composition of the board

The board has nine directors. Seven are elected from geographic districts and two are elected at large by the entire membership. The seven districts are drawn so that each contains a roughly equal number of service connections, and district boundaries are reviewed every ten years.

Directors serve three-year terms, and terms are staggered so that three seats are up for election each year. A director may serve no more than three consecutive terms, after which they must step down for at least one full term before running again.

The current board chair is Rosalind Vey, who represents District 4 and was first elected in 2017. The vice-chair is Elias Moreno, an at-large director.

## Who can run

Any member who is at least 18 years old, has been a member for at least two consecutive years, and has no overdue balance may run for the board. Employees of the cooperative and their immediate family members may not serve as directors. Candidates for a district seat must live or own property within that district.

## Nomination process

Candidates are nominated by submitting a nomination petition signed by at least 25 members. Petitions must be delivered to the headquarters no later than 5:00 p.m. on the first Friday of March. The Nominating Committee verifies the signatures and publishes the final list of candidates in the April member newsletter.

## Voting

Elections are decided by mail-in and online ballots. Ballots are sent to all members in early April and must be received by noon on the day of the Annual General Meeting, which is held on the second Saturday of May. An independent election inspector counts the ballots, and results are announced at the end of the meeting. Each member casts one vote regardless of the number of connections they hold.

## Board meetings

The board meets on the third Tuesday of every month at 18:30 in the boardroom at the headquarters. Meetings are open to members, and the first 20 minutes of each meeting are reserved for member comments. Members who wish to speak must sign up before the meeting starts and are limited to three minutes each.

## Compensation

Directors receive a stipend of 200 dollars for each board meeting they attend and 75 dollars for each committee meeting. The board chair receives an additional 1,200 dollars per year.
