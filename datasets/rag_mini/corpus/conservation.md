# Conservation Rebates

Lumen Valley Water Cooperative offers rebates to members who install water-saving fixtures and landscaping. The program, called WaterWise Valley, launched in 2012 and is funded by setting aside 1 percent of annual water sales revenue. This document lists the rebates available for the current program year, which runs from 1 January to 31 December.

## Available rebates

The following rebates are available to members in good standing whose accounts have no balance overdue by more than 30 days:

- High-efficiency toilet: 75 dollars per toilet, for models that use 4.8 litres or less per flush, up to a maximum of two toilets per connection.
- Clothes washer: 100 dollars for a high-efficiency washing machine that meets the program's water factor requirements.
- Smart irrigation controller: 120 dollars for a weather-based controller, or 50 percent of the purchase price, whichever is lower.
- Rain barrel: 25 dollars per barrel, for barrels of at least 200 litres, up to four barrels per connection.
- Lawn replacement: 2 dollars per square metre of lawn replaced with drought-tolerant planting, up to a maximum rebate of 1,500 dollars.

## How to apply

Rebate applications must be submitted within 90 days of the purchase date. Members apply through the member portal or by mailing a paper form to the headquarters. Each application must include a dated, itemised receipt and, for appliances, the model number.

For lawn replacement, a pre-inspection is required before any lawn is removed. A conservation specialist measures the area and photographs it, and then a post-inspection confirms the work after it is finished. Lawn replaced before a pre-inspection is not eligible.

## Processing and payment

Approved rebates are paid as a credit on the member's water bill, normally within two billing cycles of approval. Members who prefer a cheque may request one, but cheque rebates take up to eight weeks to arrive.

## Annual budget

The rebate budget for the current program year is 180,000 dollars. Rebates are approved on a first-come, first-served basis, and once the budget is used up, applications are placed on a waiting list for the following year.

## Free conservation kits

Separately from the rebates, any member can request a free indoor conservation kit. The kit contains a low-flow shower head, two faucet aerators, toilet leak-detection tablets and a shower timer. Kits can be picked up at the headquarters or mailed at no cost.

## Program results

Since its launch, the WaterWise Valley program has paid out more than 2,300 rebates. The cooperative estimates that fixtures and landscaping funded by the program save approximately 41 million litres of water each year.
